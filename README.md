# Tower of Arcanon

> A fantasy roguelite where every climb reveals new secrets, new powers, and new dangers.

## Overview

**Tower of Arcanon** is a fantasy roguelite focused on exploration, combat, and mastery. Players must ascend the mysterious Tower of Arcanon, a colossal magical structure filled with ancient knowledge, forgotten civilizations, powerful enemies, and unpredictable challenges.

Each run is a new attempt to reach higher floors, uncover the tower's secrets, and grow stronger through new abilities, discoveries, and character upgrades.

The tower changes with every climb — no two journeys are exactly the same.

---

## Features

### 🏰 The Tower

- Explore a mysterious ever-changing magical tower.
- Discover unique floors with different environments, enemies, and challenges.
- Uncover the history behind the tower and its creator.
- Face increasingly dangerous guardians as you ascend.

### ⚔️ Multiple Characters

Choose from a variety of characters, each with their own:

- Combat style
- Abilities
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Unique upgrades

Whether you prefer overwhelming enemies with brute force, mastering forbidden magic, or relying on agility and precision, every character offers a different way to climb.

### 🔮 Roguelite Progression

Every run is different.

- Collect temporary upgrades during your climb.
- Experiment with different builds.
- Unlock permanent improvements between attempts.
- Discover new characters, abilities, and secrets.

Failure is not the end — every attempt brings you closer to mastering the tower.

### ✨ Magic and Abilities

The Tower of Arcanon is filled with ancient magical forces.

Players can discover:

- New spells
- Unique abilities
- Powerful combinations
- Hidden interactions between skills

Create different builds and find new ways to overcome the tower's challenges.
